anderson Island — Privacy policy

About this policy

anderson Island is an independent app for ferry, nature and community information. This policy applies to version 1.0.0 and was updated November 17, 2026.

Information you provide

The app has no accounts, advertising, analytics SDKs, or developer-operated tracking service. It does not ask for your name or email address, or access your device location, contacts, microphone, camera or photo library.

Settings on your device

Display preferences are stored locally on your device. Android may include those preferences in its system backup according to your device settings. You can reset local preferences by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app.

Content requests

The app requests public information and images from Anderson Island Assistant, NOAA / National Weather Service, NOAA tides and other content providers linked by those sources. These providers receive normal network information, such as your IP address and request details, when delivering content. Their logging, retention and privacy practices are controlled by those providers. Weather and tide locations are fixed island-area coordinates, not your device location.

Websites and phone links

Website buttons open your browser. Calling links hand a telephone number to your phone app; they do not place a call automatically or share your contacts. When you visit a website, use a map, buy ferry tickets or contact an organization, that service handles any information you provide under its own privacy policy.

Retention and deletion

The developer does not operate an account database or store personal information supplied through this app. Local settings remain until you reset or remove them. Third-party content providers may retain network logs under their own policies; contact those providers for their retention and deletion practices.

Children and changes

The app is intended as a general local-information service and does not knowingly request personal information from children. This policy will be updated if the app’s data practices change.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact developer@lucas-haux.com.
